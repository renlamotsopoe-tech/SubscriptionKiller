Privacy Policy

SubscriptionKiller
Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SubscriptionKiller handles information when you use the app. SubscriptionKiller is designed to help you find subscription-related information in your email.

Information and access

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app uses Google Sign-In and Gmail read-only access to retrieve information needed to identify subscription-related emails and details, such as merchant, price, billing frequency, or dates. The app does not request Gmail permissions to send, modify, or delete emails.

Subscription-related information retrieved from Gmail is used to provide the app's subscription-discovery functionality. The app's current Gmail scan retrieves message identifiers, subjects, senders, and snippets. The information available to the app can depend on the messages it retrieves and the features you use.

How information is used

Information accessed through Gmail is used to identify and present possible subscriptions and related details within SubscriptionKiller. SubscriptionKiller does not sell users' personal information.

Third-party services

Google Sign-In and Gmail are Google services. When you connect Google, Google's handling of information is also governed by its policies. RevenueCat may be used to provide subscription purchase and entitlement services; its handling of information is described in its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can choose whether to connect a Google account. You can revoke SubscriptionKiller's access to your Google Account through your Google Account settings. You can also contact the developer with questions or requests about privacy using the address below.
